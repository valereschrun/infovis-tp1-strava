Training through data: the road to the marathon

Coursework — Information Visualization (72.74) · ITBA

In 2026 I had to cancel my original goal (Cinglés du Ventoux) and redirect all of my preparation toward the Buenos Aires Marathon on September 20th. This project explores my own training data (Strava, 2019–2026) to understand how I trained, how much, and whether I'm actually ready.

Which sport did I actually go out for the most?

Out of 234 activities logged since 2019, running clearly dominates by number of sessions (137), followed by cycling (82). The rest (snowboarding, walking, skiing, hiking) are marginal in frequency — though, as we'll see later, counting sessions can be misleading: in actual time spent training, cycling and running are much closer.
